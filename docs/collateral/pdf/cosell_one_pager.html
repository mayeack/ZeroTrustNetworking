THE PROBLEM · THREE QUESTIONS NO CUSTOMER CAN ANSWER TODAY

IDENTITY

DISCOVERY QUESTION 01

Which workloads talk to which, and is it allowed? Flow logs carry IPs, policies are written for labels, and nobody joins the two. Identity-aware flows with their verdict, in Splunk, answer it per namespace, tenant and job.

ENFORCEMENT

DISCOVERY QUESTION 02

Is the policy enforced everywhere, or just written? Kernel, DPU and switch each keep their own verdicts. One place for all three shows coverage, audit-mode flows that would be blocked, and drift from the intended policy.

FABRIC

DISCOVERY QUESTION 03

Which job owns that congested link, and what did the wait cost? Scheduler job ID to node to NIC to leaf: fabric consumption per job, and GPU-hours spent waiting on the network. Efficiency becomes a number.
