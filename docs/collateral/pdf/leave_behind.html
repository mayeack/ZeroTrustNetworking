ZERO TRUST NETWORKING · CISCO + SPLUNK

Splunk as the Control Plane for Zero Trust Networking

Cisco enforces at the kernel, the NIC and the switch. Splunk sees every flow and every policy decision, and closes the loop.

The blind spot

Zero trust is written as policy and enforced in three places: the Linux kernel, the NIC or DPU, and the switch. Each keeps its own verdicts in its own console, and none of it sits next to the identity, scheduler and security data your teams already investigate with. Nobody can say whether the policy is enforced everywhere, or which job is paying for a congested link.

What you get

Every flow with its workload identity and verdict, the process behind every connection, fabric-level enforcement events and BGP and EVPN state, joined in Splunk with job and identity context. Enterprise Security turns them into findings; SOAR and Agent Launchpad push the decision back to the nearest enforcement point, with approval and an audit trail.

The business impact

Zero trust becomes a number your teams can move: policy coverage, audit-to-enforce conversion, time to contain, and GPU-hours recovered from the fabric. New sites come up on the same design and are provable on day one, and the SOC and the network team work from the same evidence.
